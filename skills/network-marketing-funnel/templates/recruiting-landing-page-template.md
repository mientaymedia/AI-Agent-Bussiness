# Recruiting/Opportunity Landing Page — Template

Structure for a landing page introducing the business opportunity (distinct from a product sales page — see `sales-page-blueprint` for that). Fill in brackets; run the final copy through `vietnam-mlm-compliance-basics.md`'s checklist before publishing.

```markdown
## [Headline: speaks to outcome/flexibility, not product line]
Example shape: "Thêm Một Nguồn Thu Nhập, Không Cần Bỏ Công Việc Hiện Tại"
(NOT: a specific income figure, NOT "làm giàu nhanh")

## Sub-headline
[Who this is for] + [what makes it different from what they've tried/seen before]

## Problem Section
[2-3 relatable frustrations the target audience already feels]

## What This Actually Is
[Plain explanation: a product-based business model, income tied to product
volume, not recruitment alone — set correct expectations upfront, this
reduces mismatched sign-ups later]

## Why [Company/Product Category]
[Credibility: company history, product category strength, scale]
[Do not overstate — verifiable facts only]

## What You'd Be Doing
[Honest day-to-day picture: using products personally, sharing with others,
building a small team over time — avoid vague "just share on social media
and earn" framing]

## Real Starting Point (Proof)
[One or two specific, honestly-framed individual examples — clearly labeled
as individual results, not typical/expected outcomes]

## What Happens Next
[Single clear CTA: "Đăng ký để được tư vấn/gọi trao đổi trực tiếp" —
NOT "mua ngay" — enrollment happens through a real conversation]

## Lead Capture Form
Fields: Họ tên, SĐT, Zalo/Email
[No payment field on this page — this is a lead-capture step, payment/sign-up
happens after a qualifying conversation, per the 2-step funnel pattern in
offer-packaging/references/proven-launch-playbook.md]

## Required Disclaimer (Do Not Omit)
"Kết quả không điển hình. Kết quả phụ thuộc vào nỗ lực, thời gian và cam kết
cá nhân của từng người. Không có gì trên trang này là lời hứa hay đảm bảo về
thu nhập."
```

## Pre-Publish Compliance Pass

Run through `vietnam-mlm-compliance-basics.md`'s checklist in full before this goes live or receives any ad spend. Do not skip this step to save time — the legal exposure here is real, not a formality.
