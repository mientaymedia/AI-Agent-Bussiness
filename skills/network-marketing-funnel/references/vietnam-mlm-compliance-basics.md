# Vietnam MLM Compliance Basics

**This is not legal advice.** It's a working checklist to catch the most common, well-known compliance problems in multi-level marketing communications before they become legal or reputational risk. Before publishing recruiting materials at scale, have this reviewed by a lawyer familiar with Nghị định 40/2018/NĐ-CP (as amended by Nghị định 18/2023/NĐ-CP) and current Bộ Công Thương guidance — rules and enforcement practice can change, and this reference should not be treated as current legal certainty.

## Why This Matters More Than Typical Marketing Compliance

Vietnam regulates multi-level marketing (kinh doanh đa cấp) specifically and more strictly than ordinary product marketing, because the business model has a documented history of abuse (pyramid-scheme variants disguised as legitimate MLM). A compliance mistake here carries real legal exposure, not just a platform policy strike — this is a materially different risk category from the marketing-copy concerns in `copywriting` or `sales-page-blueprint`.

## Known Requirements (Verify Current Status Before Relying On These)

- **No income guarantees or income claims presented as typical.** Do not state or imply "you will earn X VND/month" or use a single success story's numbers in a way that implies it's the expected/average outcome. If citing a specific individual's result, it must be clearly framed as an individual outlier, not a projection.
- **Registration requirements for the underlying company.** The parent MLM company (Amway or otherwise) must be properly licensed to operate a multi-level marketing business in Vietnam. Henry's own distributor/IBO activity should be verified as compliant with his specific contractual terms with the company — this reference covers general marketing-communication practice, not the company-registration layer.
- **Cooling-off / right of return.** Vietnamese MLM regulation has historically required a minimum return window for new participants (commonly cited as 30 days in the regulatory framework, allowing a new distributor to return unused product/materials for a refund) — Henry's onboarding process should reflect the company's actual current policy on this, not a policy this skill invents.
- **Mandatory disclaimers on promotional content.** Content presenting the business opportunity should not create a false impression that success is guaranteed, quick, or effortless — general "results not typical, depends on effort" framing (as seen in the KOC Sách case study's disclaimer) is standard defensive practice across the info-product industry generally, and doubly important under MLM-specific scrutiny.
- **No recruitment-only compensation claims.** It should never be implied that money is earned simply from sponsoring/recruiting people — Amway's own stated model ties earnings to actual product volume (PV/BV), and communications should reflect that, both because it's accurate and because "pay for recruitment" framing is the specific pattern regulators target.

## Practical Checklist Before Publishing Any Recruiting Material

```markdown
- [ ] No specific income figure stated as an expectation, average, or guarantee
- [ ] Any named success story is clearly framed as one individual's specific result,
      not a typical/average outcome
- [ ] No claim that joining/recruiting alone generates income — ties explicitly to
      product sales/consumption (PV/BV)
- [ ] Standard disclaimer present: results vary, depend on individual effort,
      no guarantee of outcome (see the disclaimer pattern used in reference
      competitor pages already analyzed — "kết quả không điển hình")
- [ ] Return/cooling-off policy for new sign-ups matches the company's actual
      current terms, not an invented policy
- [ ] Reviewed by someone with current knowledge of Nghị định 40/2018/NĐ-CP
      and its 2023 amendment before large-scale publication
```

## What This Skill Will Not Do

This skill will not draft content that makes an income guarantee, states a specific expected earning figure as typical, or implies recruitment alone (without product volume) generates income — regardless of how the request is phrased. If asked to "make it sound like anyone can get rich fast," the correct response is to explain why that crosses into the exact territory Vietnamese MLM regulation targets, and offer a compliant alternative framing instead.

## Recommended Action Before Henry Launches At Any Scale

Given the legal exposure involved, treat "get this reviewed by a lawyer familiar with current MLM regulation" as a blocking step before any paid ad spend or large-scale content push — not an optional nice-to-have. This is explicitly flagged as a risk in the earlier strategic analysis of this decision and remains true regardless of how polished the marketing system becomes.
