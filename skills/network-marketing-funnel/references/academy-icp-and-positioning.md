# Academy ICP & 3-Track Positioning

The confirmed strategic positioning for Henry's recruiting funnel — read this before writing any invite script, ad, presentation, or content plan in this skill. Every other reference file in `network-marketing-funnel` and the network-marketing content guide in `content-creation` should target this ICP by default unless a specific request overrides it.

## The Core Positioning

**Not** a typical Amway recruiting pitch (generic "extra income" appeal to a broad audience). **Is** an AI/business-skill academy where Amway is one optional monetization track, not the headline.

```
"Học viện AI cho người làm Sales/SME" — teaches people already working in
sales, marketing, or running a small business how to use AI tools to build
additional income, with three paths students can choose from once they've
built the underlying skill.
```

This leverages Henry's actual differentiators (sales background, SaaS-for-SME building experience, ads knowledge, AI-assisted video production, "vibe coding," training ability) as the draw — not generic MLM appeal.

## ICP (Ideal Candidate Profile)

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Age | 25-40 |
| Current occupation | Sales/marketing staff at an SME, small business owner, freelancer selling a service, or someone who has done ads/content work before |
| NOT the target | General public with no sales/business background, people purely seeking passive income with zero effort, pure students/no work history |
| Why this range | Old enough to have a professional network (critical for downline growth velocity) and some existing sales/business literacy; young enough to be genuinely curious about AI rather than intimidated by it |
| Existing asset they bring | A professional network in the SME/sales/marketing space — this is what makes them valuable as a partner, not just as a customer |

## The 3-Track Model

```
Học viện "AI cho người làm Sales/SME"  ← primary brand, broad appeal
        │
        ├─→ Track 1: Use AI to build/scale a SaaS or service business
        │            (connects to Henry's own MineFlow/SaaS expertise —
        │            can feed warm leads to that business too)
        │
        ├─→ Track 2: Use AI for content + ads (general marketable skill,
        │            widest applicable audience, lowest-commitment entry point)
        │
        └─→ Track 3: Use AI to build an additional income stream via network
                     marketing (Amway) — students self-select into this,
                     it is never the default or the only path presented
```

**Why 3 tracks, not a single Amway-branded academy:** Keeps the academy's addressable audience wide (most people are open to "learn AI for sales/marketing," far fewer are open to "join my MLM team" as a first pitch). Track 3 is reached only after a student has already engaged with the academy and self-identifies interest — this is a warmer, better-qualified path into the opportunity-presentation funnel (`opportunity-presentation-structure.md`) than any cold pitch could produce.

## How This Changes the Funnel

- **Top-of-funnel content and ads** (per `content-creation/references/network-marketing-content-guide.md`) promote the *academy/skill-building* broadly — AI + sales/marketing/SME value, not Amway, not even "business opportunity" framing yet. This is also the safest framing under `paid-ads-compliant-funnel.md`'s constraints, since it isn't an MLM ad at all at this stage.
- **Track selection happens inside the academy/community**, not on the initial landing page or ad — once someone is a member and shows interest signals (asking about income streams, engaging with business-building content), they get introduced to Track 3 specifically.
- **Invite scripts and qualification** (`invite-scripts-and-qualification.md`) should be updated to reference the academy/skill-building hook first, Amway second — see that file's ICP-adjusted templates.
- **Presentation** (`opportunity-presentation-structure.md`) Problem section should open with a sales/SME-relevant frustration (e.g., "bán hàng một mình, thu nhập chạm trần dù đã dùng AI để tăng hiệu suất công việc chính") rather than a generic MLM appeal.

## What This Does NOT Change

The compliance rules in `vietnam-mlm-compliance-basics.md` and `paid-ads-compliant-funnel.md` apply identically regardless of ICP — no income guarantees, no Amway trademark in ads/landing pages, reveal at the qualification-call stage. Track 3 content specifically is still opportunity-adjacent content and must pass those checklists.
