# Paid-Ads-Compliant Funnel (De-Branded Structure)

Running paid ads for a network-marketing business hits two independent constraints that both push toward the same structural answer: **don't name the parent company in the ad, and don't lead with income/recruitment.**

## The Two Constraints

**1. Amway's own IBO Rules of Conduct** (verify against Henry's current agreement — rules are updated periodically): IBOs may not use Amway's trademarks, trade names, or logos in independent marketing materials, page titles, or ads without prior written authorization from the company, and may not distribute unauthorized Business Support Materials. This is standard across most direct-selling companies, not unique to Amway — the company protects its brand by controlling who can advertise "as" or "for" it.

**2. Meta/Facebook ad policy on MLM and business-opportunity ads**: allowed only under strict conditions — ads must not lead with earnings or recruitment framing, must not promise or imply income ("make $X/day," "replace your salary," "passive income on autopilot," guaranteed-return language are all explicitly prohibited), and should keep the ad itself focused on the product — show what it does, let the landing page carry any further disclosure. Similar restrictions apply on Google/TikTok ad platforms for business-opportunity content generally.

**The practical result:** the ad and (usually) the landing page stay de-branded and product/outcome-focused. The specific company name is revealed later in the funnel, at the qualification conversation or presentation stage — never in the ad creative, and typically not on the first landing page either.

## Where the Company Name Can and Cannot Appear

```
Ad creative (image/video/copy):        ❌ Never name Amway. Never use Amway logo/trademark.
Landing page headline/body:            ❌ Do not name Amway here either — stay outcome/product-focused.
Landing page fine print/disclaimer:    ⚠️ Verify current IBO rules before including even here.
Lead-capture confirmation (post-form): ⚠️ Case-by-case — often still withheld until a live conversation.
1:1 qualification call/message:        ✅ This is the standard reveal point.
Opportunity presentation itself:       ✅ Full company/product/compensation detail belongs here.
```

This mirrors exactly how the "Show" step in the Invite→Show→Follow-up→Launch funnel already works (see `opportunity-presentation-structure.md`) — paid ads simply feed colder traffic into the same funnel entry point a warm-market invite would, several steps earlier than the reveal.

## What the Ad CAN Say

Lead with the underlying **product benefit** (compliant, platform-safe, and doesn't touch the trademark issue at all) or a **generic outcome** (side income, flexible schedule) without naming the mechanism:

```
Product-first example shape:
"[Specific product benefit, e.g. a supplement/skincare result] — tìm hiểu thêm"
→ Safest option. No opportunity/income framing at all, no trademark issue,
  cleanly passes Meta's product-focused guidance.

Outcome-first example shape (business opportunity angle, more scrutiny):
"Đang tìm thêm 1 nguồn thu nhập linh hoạt bên cạnh công việc hiện tại?
Tìm hiểu mô hình mình đang làm — không cần bỏ việc chính."
→ Must avoid: any income figure, "guaranteed," "passive," "fast," "fire your boss"
  framing. Compliant but under tighter platform review than product-first ads.
```

## What the Ad CANNOT Say

```
❌ "Amway" / Amway logo / Amway product line names tied directly to the company
❌ Any income figure ("$500/ngày", "50 triệu/tháng") — even if true for one person
❌ "Guaranteed," "passive income," "get rich," "fire your boss," "no experience needed to earn"
❌ Screenshots of bank transfers/earnings as the ad creative itself
   (fine deeper in the funnel at the proof stage, not in top-of-funnel ad creative)
```

## Revised Funnel for Paid Traffic

```
1. AD (de-branded, product or outcome framing, Meta-compliant)
   ↓
2. LANDING PAGE (de-branded — see recruiting-landing-page-template.md,
   already structured this way by default)
   → Lead capture: name/phone/Zalo, light qualifying question
   ↓
3. QUALIFICATION TOUCH (1:1 message or short call)
   → THIS is typically where "Amway" and the specific product line get named,
   after basic interest/fit is confirmed — not before
   ↓
4. FULL PRESENTATION (per opportunity-presentation-structure.md)
   → Full detail: company, compensation model, products, next steps
```

## Ad Platform Pre-Publish Checklist

```markdown
- [ ] No Amway name, logo, or trademarked product line name anywhere in ad creative or copy
- [ ] No income figure, guarantee, or "passive/fast/easy money" language
- [ ] Ad leads with product benefit OR a generic, income-free outcome statement
- [ ] Landing page linked from the ad is also de-branded (see recruiting-landing-page-template.md)
- [ ] Reveal of company name deferred to the 1:1 qualification step, not the ad or landing page
- [ ] If running Meta ads specifically: review current Meta Advertising Standards for
      multi-level marketing before publishing — policy specifics change and should be
      re-checked at time of campaign launch, not assumed from this reference
- [ ] If in doubt about current Amway IBO advertising rules, confirm with the company
      or Henry's upline before spending ad budget — a violation can affect IBO status,
      which is a materially higher-stakes risk than a rejected ad
```

## Why De-Branded Is Not a Workaround — It's the Correct Structure

This isn't "hiding" the business — it mirrors the standard, compliant approach used broadly across the direct-selling industry for paid acquisition, precisely because both the platform rules and the parent company's own rules independently converge on it. A prospect who clicks a product-benefit or generic-opportunity ad and later learns the specific company at a qualification call is a completely normal, expected funnel shape — not a bait-and-switch, as long as the ad's actual claims (whatever framing used) are honest and platform-compliant.
