# Network Marketing Funnel Skill

## When to Use

Activate this skill when:
- Building an "opportunity" funnel to invite prospects into a network-marketing business (distinct from a product sales page — see `sales-page-blueprint` for that)
- Writing invite scripts for warm-market (personal network) or social-media outreach
- Structuring a business-opportunity presentation (in-person, Zoom, or recorded video)
- Designing prospect qualification so time is spent on serious candidates, not everyone
- Checking marketing copy for Vietnam MLM legal compliance before publishing

This is the "Attract new team members" layer — pairs with `network-marketing-crm` (which tracks prospects once captured) and `offer-packaging`'s `proven-launch-playbook.md` (if packaging the opportunity itself as a structured paid training rather than a direct sponsor pitch).

## The Classic Network-Marketing Funnel: Invite → Show → Follow-Up → Launch

This 4-step structure is the industry-standard pattern (independently converged on by every network-marketing organization, not specific to Amway) — use it as the default shape:

```
1. INVITE — a short, curiosity-driven message to a warm contact or social audience,
   asking for permission to share something, not pitching in the invite itself

2. SHOW — the actual business-opportunity presentation (live, Zoom, or a recorded
   video) where the model, products, and income opportunity are explained properly

3. FOLLOW-UP — the single highest-leverage, most commonly skipped step. Most
   prospects need 2-5 touches after seeing the presentation before deciding.

4. LAUNCH — onboarding a new IBO the moment they say yes — fast, clear next steps,
   first 72 hours matter most for retention (see network-marketing-crm's
   "first 30 days" check-in cadence)
```

## Critical Rule: Never Skip the Qualification Step

The single biggest time-waster in network marketing is showing the full presentation to everyone, regardless of fit. Use the qualification questions in `invite-scripts-and-qualification.md` **before** committing to a full 45-60 minute presentation — a 5-minute qualifying conversation saves hours over a month of outreach.

## Landing Page for the Opportunity (If Building One)

If packaging the recruiting funnel as a landing page (rather than only 1:1 invites), it needs a different structure than a product sales page:

- Headline speaks to the **outcome** (income flexibility, being your own boss) not the product line
- **Never name Amway or use its trademark/logo on the page** — this applies whether traffic is paid or organic, per `paid-ads-compliant-funnel.md`; the reveal happens in a live qualification conversation, not on the page
- Never state or imply a specific income figure as typical or guaranteed (see compliance reference — this is a legal requirement, not just good practice)
- CTA is "learn more" / "request a call" — not "buy now." The actual enrollment always happens through a real conversation, not a checkout page
- Use `templates/recruiting-landing-page-template.md` as the starting structure

## Running Paid Ads (Read Before Spending Budget)

Paid traffic (Meta, TikTok, Google) is a valid channel for this funnel, but it is **more constrained than organic invites** — both Amway's own IBO advertising rules and the ad platforms' MLM/business-opportunity policies restrict what can appear in an ad. Read `references/paid-ads-compliant-funnel.md` in full before writing or publishing a single ad — the short version: de-branded, product-or-outcome framing only, no income claims, no Amway name/logo, reveal the company at the qualification-call stage.

## Usage Examples

**Invite Script**:
```
Write 3 warm-market invite message variations for reaching out to former
colleagues about the business opportunity, casual Zalo tone.
```

**Qualification Call**:
```
Give me 5 qualifying questions to ask before inviting someone to a full
presentation, so I don't waste a slot on someone not seriously interested.
```

**Presentation Structure**:
```
Outline a 30-minute Zoom opportunity presentation for [product category],
following the problem → credibility → model → proof → next-step arc.
```

**Compliance Check**:
```
Review this invite script/landing page draft for Vietnam MLM compliance
issues before I publish it: [paste text]
```
