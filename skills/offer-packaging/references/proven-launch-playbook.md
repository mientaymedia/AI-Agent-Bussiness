# Proven Launch Playbook

A synthesis of the recurring structural pattern found across three independently-run Vietnamese info-product launches analyzed as case studies (an AI-tooling kit, a lifestyle-business course, and a KOC/affiliate course). These three offers differ completely in niche, but converge on the same underlying mechanics — strong evidence this is a **proven, repeatable launch formula**, not a one-off tactic. Use this as the default structure when packaging a new offer, including a knowledge/training offer built around a network-marketing or affiliate business.

**This is an original synthesis of structural patterns (pricing psychology, offer architecture, funnel mechanics) — not copied copy from any source.** Do not paste competitor headlines or testimonial text verbatim into a new offer; use the *pattern*, write original content.

## The 8 Recurring Mechanics

### 1. Price Anchor at ~75-84% Off, Landing in the 700K-999K VND Band
Every case studied anchors a "regular price" (3.9M-4.9M VND) against a launch price that lands just under a round number (789K, 790K, 980K). This band is low enough to be an impulse buy for a Vietnamese digital-product buyer, high enough to filter out completely non-committed browsers, and the discount percentage (>75%) reads as "too good to pass up" without looking free.

```
Formula: Anchor price = Launch price / (1 - discount%), discount% in 75-84% range
Example: Launch price 790,000đ → Anchor price ≈ 790,000 / 0.20 ≈ 3,950,000đ
```

**Caution:** an anchor price with no evidence it was ever actually charged is a credibility risk if a buyer checks. Where possible, anchor against a real prior price (e.g. an actual earlier-cohort price) rather than a fabricated "original price."

### 2. Program Structure: Fixed-Length Challenge OR Numbered Modules
Two variants observed, pick based on what suits the content:
- **X-Day Challenge** (used by the AI-tooling and lifestyle-business cases, 7 and 14 days): daily unlocks, a named theme per day, builds momentum and daily habit — best when the content benefits from sequential, cumulative practice.
- **N-Module Course** (used by the KOC/affiliate case, 5 modules): no day-based pacing, learner-paced — best when steps don't need to happen on a fixed calendar (e.g. content-creation skill building).

Either way: **every phase/day/module gets a short, punchy name** (not "Lesson 3" but "THE OFFER — Tìm & Đóng Gói Sản Phẩm Đáng Tiền"), and phases build in a clear narrative arc: mindset/awareness → core skill-building → execution/monetization.

### 3. Bonus Stack: 4-6 Items, Each With a Stated VND Value
Every case stacks bonuses with explicit VND values (never "several bonuses," always itemized with a number), totaling well above the offer price (5M-27M VND stacked against a 790K-980K price). At least one bonus is marked "vô giá" (priceless) — almost always the community/ongoing-access item, since it can't be assigned a believable fixed number.

```
Formula: Total bonus value ≈ 6-30x the launch price
Bonus types that recur: (1) a workbook/template, (2) recurring live training,
(3) community access, (4) a named AI assistant/agent, (5) a done-for-you
asset (scripts, source lists), (6) a discount on the seller's higher-tier offer
```

### 4. Guarantee: Short Window, Conditional on Completed Work (Not Unconditional)
Two of three cases have a guarantee; both attach it to **proof of effort**, not a no-questions-asked refund:
- 3-day window, must complete ≥3 tasks (AI-tooling case)
- 30-day window, must complete ≥10/14 days + submit an offer + stay active in community (lifestyle-business case)

This is deliberate: it filters out refund-tourism while still reducing purchase anxiety. The KOC/affiliate case has **no guarantee at all** — viable when the price is low enough (980K) that the guarantee's risk-reduction value matters less than avoiding refund-abuse overhead.

**Recommendation for a new offer:** default to a conditional guarantee (window + minimum task completion), only skip it if price is under ~1M VND and cash flow can't absorb refund-processing overhead.

### 5. Named AI Assistant as a Community Support Layer
Two of three cases embed a **named AI agent persona** inside the buyer community (Telegram/Zalo) — "AI Agent Kong," "AI Agent Mây" — answering questions 24/7. This is a distinct pattern worth replicating: it reduces the founder's personal support burden while feeling more personal than a generic bot, because it's named and modeled on the founder's own voice/expertise.

### 6. Proof Pattern: One Flagship Case With Verifiable Numbers
Every case leads with **one named, specific success story with real numbers** (not "many students succeeded" — a named person, a platform screenshot, a dollar/VND figure, a timeframe). Generic testimonials appear too, but the flagship case gets the most prominent placement and the most specific data.

```
Formula: [Name] + [starting situation] + [specific platform/method] +
[specific number: revenue/orders/results] + [specific timeframe]
```

### 7. Urgency: Countdown Timer + Stated Limited Slots
All three use a visible countdown and/or a "only N of 1000 slots left at this price" counter. Treat this as standard practice, but **only use a real constraint** — a fabricated countdown that resets on every page load is a known trust-destroying pattern if a buyer notices (e.g. revisits the page a day later and sees the same 23:59 remaining).

### 8. Two-Step Funnel: Capture Contact Info, Then Route to Payment
None of the cases put a payment button as the very first CTA. The flow is always: landing page → lead capture form (name/phone/email, sometimes with qualifying questions) → redirect to a separate checkout/payment step. This lets the seller follow up with anyone who drops off before paying (via the win-back sequence pattern in `delivery-setup-guide`).

## Applying This Playbook to a New Offer

Use this checklist when packaging any new offer with this formula (including a network-marketing/affiliate training offer):

```markdown
## Launch Playbook Worksheet: [Offer Name]

1. Anchor price: ___________ → Launch price: ___________ (discount %: ___)
   Is the anchor price real or illustrative? ___________

2. Structure: [ ] X-Day Challenge  [ ] N-Module Course
   Phase names (short, punchy, one per day/module):
   1. _______________
   2. _______________
   ...

3. Bonus stack (4-6 items, each with VND value):
   1. _______________ — Giá trị: ___________
   2. _______________ — Giá trị: ___________
   ...
   Total stacked value: ___________ (target: 6-30x launch price)

4. Guarantee: Window ___ days, condition: complete ≥___ of ___ [tasks/days]
   (or: no guarantee, justified because price is under 1M VND)

5. AI assistant persona name: ___________
   Modeled on: [founder's voice / a specific expert persona]

6. Flagship proof case:
   Name: ___________ Starting situation: ___________
   Method/platform: ___________ Result (number + timeframe): ___________

7. Urgency mechanism: [ ] Countdown  [ ] Limited slots (real number: ___)
   Constraint is real, not fabricated: [ ] confirmed

8. Funnel steps: Landing page → Lead form (fields: _______) → Payment page
   Drop-off follow-up: link to win-back-reengagement-sequence.md
```

## Why This Matters for a Network-Marketing / Affiliate Offer Specifically

If the underlying business is network marketing (recruiting a downline) or affiliate-style selling, this playbook is best used to package **the training/system that teaches the skill**, not to make MLM income claims themselves. Two important adjustments:

- The "flagship proof case" must show a **specific, verifiable activity result** (content posted, orders processed, a concrete skill demonstrated) — not an income promise. Vietnamese law (Nghị định 40/2018/NĐ-CP, amended by Nghị định 18/2023/NĐ-CP) prohibits multi-level marketing communications that promise or guarantee income, and requires clear disclaimers that results are not typical. See `network-marketing-funnel/references/vietnam-mlm-compliance-basics.md`.
- The guarantee condition should require **actual outbound activity** (contacted N prospects, posted N pieces of content, ran the qualification script N times) rather than just consuming video content — this both filters refund-tourism and pre-trains the actual behavior the business needs.
