Build the recruiting/enrollment funnel for a network-marketing (Amway-style) business.

<args>$ARGUMENTS</args>

## Steps
- `invite [channel]` - Generate warm-market or social invite script variations
- `qualify` - Generate qualification questions for filtering prospects
- `presentation [format]` - Outline an opportunity presentation (live/Zoom/recorded)
- `landing-page` - Build a de-branded recruiting/opportunity landing page (not a product sales page)
- `paid-ads [platform]` - Build de-branded ad creative/copy compliant with Amway's IBO rules and the platform's MLM ad policy
- `compliance-check` - Review draft copy against Vietnam MLM compliance rules AND paid-ads/trademark rules

## Process
1. Activate Attraction Agent
2. Use Network Marketing Funnel skill
3. Follow the Invite → Show → Follow-up → Launch structure
4. For `paid-ads` or `landing-page`: apply the de-branded structure from `references/paid-ads-compliant-funnel.md` — no Amway name/logo, no income claims, product-or-outcome framing only, reveal the company at the qualification-call stage
5. Run all opportunity-facing copy through both the Vietnam MLM compliance checklist and the paid-ads checklist before finalizing
6. Output: Ready-to-use script, presentation outline, ad copy, or landing page copy — flagged for compliance issues if any

## Examples
- `/network-funnel invite zalo` — 3 warm-market invite message variations for Zalo
- `/network-funnel qualify` — 5 qualifying questions to filter before a full presentation
- `/network-funnel presentation zoom` — 25-30 minute Zoom opportunity presentation outline
- `/network-funnel landing-page` — De-branded opportunity landing page, company name withheld until the qualification call
- `/network-funnel paid-ads meta` — De-branded Meta ad copy variations (product-first and outcome-first), Amway name/logo never used
- `/network-funnel compliance-check` — Review a draft ad/landing page/script for MLM + trademark + platform-ad-policy issues

## Tips
- Never skip qualification — a 5-minute filter saves hours of wasted presentations
- Any income figure must be framed as one individual's specific result, never typical/expected
- Never name Amway or use its logo in ad creative or the landing page — reveal the company at the qualification-call stage, not before (both Amway's IBO rules and ad-platform MLM policy require this)
- Get legal review before any paid ad spend on recruiting content — this is a blocking step, not optional
